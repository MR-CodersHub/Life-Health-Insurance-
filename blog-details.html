05 • ACTION FRAMEWORK

5 Essential Steps Before You Sign or Purchase

Follow this fiduciary checklist to guarantee you avoid common underwriting mistakes and hidden exclusions.

Checklist Progress 1 of 5 Completed (20%)
Step 1: Calculate Your Household's True Economic Gap

Use the DIME method (Debt, Income, Mortgage, Education) instead of picking an arbitrary round figure.

Step 2: Disclose Complete Medical History Transparently

Full transparency prevents statutory contestability disputes and guarantees uncompromised claims settlements.

Step 3: Audit Hospital & Preferred Physician Network Tiers

Confirm your local medical facilities are classified as In-Network to eliminate out-of-pocket surprise billings.

Step 4: Select Critical Illness & Premium Waiver Riders

A waiver-of-premium rider keeps your policy active if you become disabled and cannot pay premiums.

Step 5: Name Both Primary and Contingent Beneficiaries

Always specify contingent beneficiaries to ensure insurance proceeds bypass lengthy probate court delays.
