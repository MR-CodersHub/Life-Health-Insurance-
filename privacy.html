01

Commitment to Privacy & Fiduciary Standard

WellSure Insurance Brokerage LLC ("WellSure", "we", "our", or "us") operates as an independent insurance intermediary. In order to compare policies, calculate actuarial premiums, submit applications to certified underwriters, and advocate on your behalf during claims, we handle sensitive personal, financial, and medical records.

We view our data obligations through a strict fiduciary lens: your private information is held in confidence under statutory compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, IRDAI Cyber Security Framework, and international privacy benchmarks.

Our Ironclad Zero-Sale Guarantee:

WellSure will never sell, lease, rent, or trade your personal email, phone number, address, health records, or family identities to third-party marketing companies, ad networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.

03

Lawful Basis & Purposes of Data Processing

We process your personal and sensitive data strictly under legitimate legal bases recognized by applicable data privacy regulations:

  • Performance of Contract: Facilitating policy proposals, comparing plan rates, booking advisory meetings, and managing your ongoing coverage via the Client Dashboard.
  • Consent: When you voluntarily submit diagnostic files, request tele-medical coordination, or consent to policy renewal SMS/email reminders.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Retaining mandatory broker transaction audit trails, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verifications, and reporting to statutory bodies like the IRDAI.
  • Fiduciary Claims Advocacy: Sharing medical records and hospital billing files with TPAs and insurer claims committees to secure cashless pre-authorizations or contest arbitrary deductions.
04

Sharing & Disclosure Safeguards

WellSure only discloses your private data to authorized entities essential for servicing your insurance policy:

  • Selected Insurance Carriers: When you apply for a policy, your proposal form and medical records are transmitted directly to the underwriting department of that specific carrier (e.g., Care Health, HDFC Life, Star Health, Tata AIG).
  • Licensed Third Party Administrators (TPAs) & Hospitals: During a cashless hospitalization claim, your e-card number and diagnostic summaries are shared with the hospital desk and assigned TPA to process cashless approvals.
  • Accredited Diagnostic Laboratories: For mandatory pre-policy medical checkups, limited contact and booking information is coordinated with accredited diagnostic centers.
  • Statutory Authorities: If required by court subpoena, tax authority, or insurance ombudsman directive under applicable penal statutes.

Every carrier and vendor partnering with WellSure is contractually bound by rigorous non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and statutory data protection protocols.

05

Data Storage, 256-Bit SSL Encryption & Retention

Security is foundational to our brokerage infrastructure. All electronic transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) ciphers.

  • Data at Rest: Customer databases, medical uploads, and policy bonds stored in the Client Vault are encrypted using AES-256 with hardware security module (HSM) key rotations.
  • Access Control: Administrative access follows the principle of least privilege (PoLP) and requires hardware token multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Data Retention Timelines: In compliance with insurance regulatory laws, active policy records are retained for the lifetime of the policy plus 7 years post-termination. Quotation enquiries that do not materialize into active policies are purged after 24 months unless renewed by client consent.
06

Your Statutory Rights & Data Choices

Under the DPDP Act and international data governance laws, you hold the following rights regarding your personal records held by WellSure:

  • Right of Access & Summary: View all personal data, nominee details, and uploaded files anytime via the Client Dashboard Document Vault.
  • Right of Correction: Request immediate rectification of inaccurate contact details, nominee changes, or address updates.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: If you choose not to proceed with an insurance proposal, you may submit a written request to delete your profile and medical records from our active CRM databases.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may opt out of non-essential communications (such as monthly educational newsletters) via one-click unsubscribe links or dashboard notification toggles.
07

Medical & Diagnostic Records Safeguards

We treat all medical documentation (discharge summaries, diagnostic imaging, physician notes, and laboratory results) as Special Category Sensitive Personal Data (SCSPD).

Medical files are stored in isolated, access-restricted repositories. Staff advisors are trained on patient confidentiality protocols, and records are released to carrier underwriters strictly with your explicit digital authorization.

08

Cookies, Session Tokens & Tracking Technologies

The WellSure website uses cookies and browser local storage to deliver secure, responsive services:

  • Strictly Essential Cookies: Required to maintain secure authenticated sessions in the Client Portal and Admin Dashboard.
  • Preference Storage: Stores your Dark/Light theme mode, RTL reading preferences, and temporary quote parameters so you don't lose progress.
  • Privacy-Friendly Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized telemetry used strictly to identify slow page load times and broken links. We do not use intrusive cross-site fingerprinting or behavioural tracking scripts.
09

Minors & Nominee Privacy Protections

Where an insurance policy covers minor children (such as Family Floater health plans or child education savings policies), data regarding minors is collected exclusively from the verified parent or legal guardian.

We never engage in profiling or direct marketing targeted at minors. Information concerning minor beneficiaries is used exclusively for coverage determination and claim proceeds disbursement to designated legal guardians.
