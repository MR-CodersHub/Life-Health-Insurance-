04 • COST DRIVERS

What Determines Your Insurance Premium?

Insurance underwriters evaluate specific actuarial criteria to price risk accurately. Understanding these factors helps you make smarter decisions.

Transparent Underwriting Insights
We explain how carrier actuaries calculate rates so you never encounter hidden surprises.

Age & Life Stage

Age and life circumstances can influence insurance pricing. Securing coverage earlier locks in favorable baseline brackets.

Coverage Amount

The amount of protection selected affects the premium. Higher sums insured provide deeper safety but require proportionate premiums.

Policy Duration

Coverage period and policy structure matter. Level term periods (10, 20, or 30 years) impact long-term pricing commitments.

Health & Lifestyle

Health history and underwriting factors may influence premiums. Routine vitals and non-smoking status unlock preferred rate discounts.
