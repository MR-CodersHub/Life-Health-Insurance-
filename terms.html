Statutory Status Notice:

WellSure is an insurance intermediary representing the policyholder. We are not an insurance underwriting company and do not underwrite balance-sheet insurance risk directly. Policies recommended by WellSure are legally underwritten and issued by licensed third-party insurance carriers.

02

Nature of Advisory Scope & Fiduciary Standard

Our advisors act as fiduciary consultants. This means our primary legal allegiance is to you—the client. Our scope of brokerage services encompasses:

  • Comprehensive assessment of family income replacement targets, human life value (HLV), and catastrophic healthcare needs.
  • Unbiased cross-comparison of coverage terms, waiting periods, sub-limits, exclusions, and claim settlement ratios (CSR) across 40+ accredited insurance carriers.
  • Assistance in accurately completing digital proposal forms and compiling required medical documentation.
  • Dedicated post-issuance policy administration, nominee record updating, and claim advocacy.

While our advisors provide expert guidance based on published carrier rate cards and underwriting guidelines, the final decision to choose an insurance plan, sum insured, and deductible rests exclusively with the policyholder.

03

Premium Quotations & Carrier Underwriting Disclosures

All premium figures, benefit illustrations, cash value projections, and tax deduction estimates displayed on our website, calculators, or marketing materials are strictly indicative. Final premium charges are established exclusively by the underwriting department of the selected carrier following:

  • Tele-medical verification and review of historical diagnostic records.
  • Actuarial evaluation of age, occupation, tobacco/nicotine consumption, family medical history, and body mass index (BMI).
  • Application of statutory Goods and Services Tax (GST) or applicable state insurance excise duties.

Should a carrier impose an underwriting counter-offer (loading premium or applying specific medical exclusions), WellSure will immediately notify you with full transparent disclosures, allowing you to accept or reject the proposal without penalty.

04

Client Duty of Utmost Good Faith (Uberrima Fides)

Insurance contracts are legally founded on the doctrine of Uberrima Fides (Utmost Good Faith). When applying for life or health coverage through WellSure, you warrant that all information provided is accurate, truthful, and complete.

Crucial Disclosure Requirement:

Non-disclosure, misstatement, or concealment of material facts (including past surgeries, pre-existing chronic conditions like diabetes or hypertension, smoking habits, or rejected prior insurance proposals) entitles the insurance carrier to void the policy ab initio and repudiate claims under statutory Section 45 provisions.

WellSure advisors are strictly prohibited from encouraging or colluding in non-disclosure. We will always counsel full disclosure to ensure your family's claims are paid without legal jeopardy.

05

Policy Inception, Free-Look Period & Cancellations

Coverage commences only once the underwriter formally issues the policy bond and assigns an official Policy Number. Payment of an initial application deposit does not constitute immediate binding coverage until the underwriter's formal acceptance.

Every policyholder enjoys a statutory Free-Look Period from the date of physical or electronic delivery of the policy document. During this window, you have the unconditional legal right to review the policy terms and cancel the coverage if dissatisfied:

Policy Type Statutory Free-Look Window Refund Terms
Electronic / Online Policies 30 Calendar Days 100% Premium Refund (less proportionate risk cover & medical stamp duty)
Physical Offline Policies 15 Calendar Days 100% Premium Refund (less proportionate risk cover & medical stamp duty)
Renewals & Endorsements N/A (Continuous Coverage) Cancellation governed by carrier short-period refund scale

To initiate a Free-Look cancellation, submit a written request via your Client Dashboard or contact your assigned WellSure advisor within the specified timeframe.

06

Premium Payments, Grace Periods & Policy Lapses

All premium remittances must be paid directly to the licensed insurance company through verified banking channels, electronic payment gateways (UPI, NEFT, Net Banking, Credit/Debit Cards), or authorized carrier NACH e-mandates. WellSure and its advisors never collect cash premiums on personal accounts.

  • Grace Period: Carriers provide a standard grace period of 30 days (15 days for monthly mode) for payment of renewal premiums. Coverage remains active during the grace period.
  • Policy Lapse: If premium remains unpaid upon expiry of the grace period, the policy lapses. Reviving a lapsed policy requires carrier approval, submission of a Good Health Declaration, and potential medical re-examination.
07

Claims Advocacy & Assistance Standards

WellSure maintains a dedicated 24/7 Fiduciary Claims Advocacy Desk. When a covered event occurs (hospitalization, critical illness diagnosis, or death), our specialists assist you and your designated nominees with:

  • Expediting cashless pre-authorization with Third Party Administrators (TPAs) and network hospital desks.
  • Auditing claim dossiers, discharge summaries, and medical billing items to prevent arbitrary carrier deductions.
  • Escalating contested or delayed claims to carrier Senior Grievance Committees.
Adjudication Clarification:

The legal authority to admit, quantify, or reject an insurance claim resides exclusively with the underwriting insurance carrier pursuant to the policy contract. WellSure does not have contractual power to disburse insurance payouts directly, but will vigorously represent your legal rights in disputed claims.
